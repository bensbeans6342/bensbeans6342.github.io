NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between the administration of chudmaxxing (the "Disclosing Party") and the undersigned member (the "Receiving Party").

1. Scope of Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes all discussions, media, messages, and content shared within any channel of the chudmaxxing Discord server. This applies to all public text channels, restricted channels, voice channels, and related direct messages.

2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to maintain the strict confidentiality of all server content. The Receiving Party shall not:

  • Share, copy, export, or screenshot any messages, documents, or media from the server to external platforms or individuals.
  • Stream, record, or broadcast server activities without explicit permission from the Disclosing Party.
  • Disclose personal information, user activity, or server discussions to anyone not currently holding membership in the server.

3. Term and Survivability

The duty to protect Confidential Information remains in effect indefinitely. This obligation continues even if the Receiving Party leaves the server voluntarily, is kicked, or is banned.

4. Destruction of Materials

Upon termination of server membership, the Receiving Party must permanently delete all saved or downloaded Confidential Information, including chat logs, screenshots, and media files originating from the server.

5. Consequences of Breach

Any unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information will result in an immediate and permanent ban from the server. The Disclosing Party reserves the right to report the Receiving Party to Discord Trust and Safety and communicate the breach to partner communities for blacklisting purposes.

6. Acknowledgment

By joining the server or sending a message, you affirm that you (or your legal guardian if you are below the age of 18) have received this Non-Disclosure Agreement and will follow it with the terms stated.
